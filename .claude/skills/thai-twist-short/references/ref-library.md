## Patterns shared by all four

1. **Hook = a scenario, not a mood.** "ถ้าซอมบี้บุกโรงเรียน", "ไฟดับทั้งกรุงเทพฯ", "อย่าขับเข้าเมือง". Concrete place + concrete threat in the first line.
2. **Knowledge you can use.** Every 3–5 shots give a real fact, rule or number: น้ำค้างท่อ, 5 คน/ตร.ม., จมูกหมา 300 ล้านตัวรับกลิ่น, ประตูเปิดเข้า/ออก. The viewer feels smarter, which is why they watch to the end and share.
3. **Rules on cards.** "กฎข้อ 1 อย่าวิ่งลงบันไดตามเพื่อน", "Farm rule #2". Numbered rules give the clip a spine and a reason to keep counting.
4. **Numbers that move.** Day counters (วันที่ 1), clocks (13:53, 02:00), water level %, a stat that ticks 11 → 79 → 90 ลิตร, a calendar "อยู่ได้ 27 วัน", a thermometer 33 → 36 °C. A number changes on screen at least every ~10 s.
5. **Diagrams.** Cross-sections (pump → pipe → floor 23), top-down crowd density, door hinge open-in vs open-out, map with distance rings. Diagrams are the most-shared frames.
6. **Named, recurring cast + one animal.** เจย์ + แมวส้มโอ, ต้น / ฟ้า-หัวหน้าห้อง + เจ้าด่าง, Eli + his dog. Name tags with a coloured dot label people the first time they appear.
7. **Bright sets exist.** Two of four are mostly daylight. Darkness is saved for the turn, so it means something.
8. **Close-ups.** Dog nose, a sweating face, a bandaged arm, a hand on a tap, a generator filling the frame. Roughly 1 in 4 shots is a close-up or a single object.
9. **Depth.** Soft-focus background + vignette; foreground props cross the frame.
10. **Clues are physical and fair.** Long sleeves in 36 °C heat + a classroom thermometer → bitten. The clue is on screen 3+ times before it is named.
11. **Captions are mostly white.** Colour goes on **one keyword** (gold for facts/numbers, red for danger). Full red boxes only for the 2–4 lines that turn the story.
12. **Series ending.** Three of four end on a cliffhanger line, then a "ตอนต่อไป / ตอนหน้า" card with the next title as a question and a short follow line. The twist answers this episode; the card opens the next.

## Pacing

- Survival clips cut every **2.5–3 s** (ref "ถ้าซอมบี้": 23 cuts in 70 s) and add a card, counter or tick inside long shots.
- The folklore clip holds shots for 5–30 s with a slow camera, rain and lamp flicker; it works because the creature reveal is huge and late. Use this slow mode only for `ตำนาน` stories.
- Lengths: 61–130 s. Survival 60–110 s, folklore up to ~130 s.
